About these demonstrations

These are independent projects hosted on Render. Free-tier services may sleep after inactivity, so the first request can take around a minute while a service starts. This page checks each service in the background and updates its status separately.

CaseFlow and VeriFHIR are proof-of-concept demonstrations; Commonthread uses synthetic fundraising data. Their demonstrations are intended to show technical approaches, not to represent production legal, clinical, or institutional systems.
